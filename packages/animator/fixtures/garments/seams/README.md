# Arm/torso seam masks (seam-darkness check)

Masks for `scripts/polish-seam-ink.py check <texture> <mask> --rect <paint rect> [--ink r,g,b]`, one per template, on
the 1086 x 1448 canvas (white = seam band). Generated by `scripts/swag/seam-mask.ts` from a domain on the committed body
rig ():

    npx tsx scripts/swag/seam-mask.ts fixtures/garments/stock-cloth.domain.json fixtures/garments/seams/simple-arm-seam-mask.png
    npx tsx scripts/swag/seam-mask.ts fixtures/garments/medium-gambeson.domain.json fixtures/garments/seams/medium-arm-seam-mask.png

How: the rest footprints of the torso and each upper-arm group are rasterised. Their interface is smoothed along its
rows, moved `--outset` px (default 6) toward the sleeve, drawn 6 px wide and kept 8 px inside the silhouette.

The outset is calibrated on the simple template against the earlier hand-made mask `.scratch/r16/arm-seam-mask.png`
(the leather r14 drawn seam minus the structural armpit part). Outset 6 has the best overlap (IoU 0.40, 75% of r16
within 2 px). It keeps the verdicts:

| Texture | r16 mask | simple (generated) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| leather r14 control | FAIL 17.0 / 7.4 | FAIL 14.0 / 7.4 |
| leather r15 | PASS 1.7 / 7.2 | PASS 1.8 / 7.2 |
| Wanderer's Wrap v1 (`--ink 36,10,10`) | PASS 2.1 / 7.0 | PASS 2.5 / 7.0 |
| jacket-clean (`--ink 53,15,2`) | PASS 4.8 / 6.2 | PASS 5.5 / 6.2 |

Without the outset (the raw interface, about 6 px inboard of the drawn seams), the leather control passed (5.8). So the
outset is part of the generator, not a tuning knob per item.

## Per-material reading: `--domain`

    python scripts/polish-seam-ink.py check <texture> <mask> --rect L,T,W,H --domain fixtures/garments/<item>.domain.json

The domain's `seamCheck` block holds the item's outline `ink` and its materials by nominal base colour, each marked
`"pattern": "plain"` or `"dark"`:

- `dark`: a material whose own pattern is dark line work: mail rings, lamellar plate rows, laminar arm lames. Its
  ring gaps and row shadows are the material, not shading along the seam. Texels within 4 px of paint whose nearest
  configured material is `dark` are read in the default mode (luminance > 60 is interior).
- `plain`: cloth, leather, lacquered or bronze plates without a dark pattern. Read with the ink override (only texels
  within 30 RGB of the outline ink are line work), so a near-black plain fabric is still measured.

`--ink` on the command line overrides the block's ink colour; without `--domain` the override applies everywhere as
before (jacket, Wrap v1). Configured: `chinese-jade-guard` (mail dark; jade, bronze, gold, leather plain),
`chinese-mirror-plate` (red lamellar and steel arm lames dark; gold plain), `chinese-wrap` (all plain).
Tests: `src/tools/seamDarkness.test.ts` (mail compressing toward the seam fails under the global override and passes
per material; a shade band on the mail and a smudge on the plain cloth still fail).
